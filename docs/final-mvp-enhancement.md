## Collection and visibility policy

- **Mode** is Personal or Crowdsourced, chosen at creation and immutable. Existing projects default to Crowdsourced; no previous JSON definition is rewritten.
- **Visibility** is Private or Public, independent of mode. Visibility is derived from an active explicit dataset publication, not from collection lifecycle status. There is no selected-person sharing.
- Personal collections start Private, immediately freeze their initial criteria version and become capture-ready without reserving or spending QC. Their internal lifecycle state is Published (active), which does **not** mean publicly visible. Their collection-detail and image APIs remain owner-only even after a separately authorised public dataset is published.
- Personal creation offers one required label question with 2–30 touch choices. Advanced setup reuses the existing five-type criteria builder; added questions now default to optional. Existing required questions remain required. Once capture-ready, the schema/licence/reuse terms remain frozen; per-image personal label values may be edited with immutable audit revisions. Different schema or terms require a new collection.
- The stored `reward_per_image` field remains the community reward setting and is ignored for Personal mode. Personal budgets and actual rewards are always zero; database guards prohibit any collection-linked ledger entry or reservation for a personal project. UI surfaces display 0 QC.
- Public publication retains all existing separate rights gates: explicit licensed owner publication policy, per-image distribution consent, and owner rights/privacy authorisation. Owners use these same explicit gates for their own images. Public publication never enables community submissions to Personal collections.
- Existing terminal withdrawal policy remains: withdrawing an image's distribution consent or a whole publication stops further distribution and cannot be silently undone. Previously downloaded copies cannot be recalled. A withdrawn policy cannot be republished in this MVP; use a new collection/permission lifecycle rather than resetting consent history.
- Publicly authorised personal images must have their distribution permission withdrawn before relabelling. This avoids silently changing the data covered by an earlier privacy review. Previously published manifests are immutable; withdrawal revokes affected distributions. A withdrawn image remains privately editable but cannot reuse its terminal grant.

## Owner images and funding

Owners use a distinct backend-inferred image workflow (stored `workflow_version=2`; API `source=owner`). Clients cannot choose the workflow, approval state, reward or reviewer. Validated owner images become Approved without a community moderation decision, notification or reward entry. Community submissions remain workflow 1, Pending Review until an authorised decision, with the existing exactly-once reservation-funded payout.

Both owner upload slots and community uploads/pending/approved images consume target capacity. Suppressed owner images cease consuming capacity; community approval history retains its original credit/capacity semantics. Project row locks serialise slot allocation, owner completion, community decisions and closure. Retrying an allocation key after waiting for this lock rechecks the existing record, avoiding duplicate records under contention. Per-owner sequential captures do not inherit the community 20-image limit; the five-active-upload and project target limits still apply.

**Conservative funding policy:** a crowdsourced project reserves its original full target × reward on publication. Adding an owner image reduces available community target capacity but does not partially refund escrow. Existing pending and active community uploads cannot be displaced. Surplus QC stays held until closure, when the existing append-only release entry returns precisely the unspent amount. Removing an owner image can reopen capacity while the original reservation remains available; it never recreates a released reservation. This deliberately avoids partial-refund/top-up semantics in the existing immutable reservation model.

Owners may capture in Published or Paused projects with a live reservation, subject to closing dates and capacity. Community capture requires Published. Closed collections accept no new owner or community images. Community reviews remain allowed while Paused and prohibited after closure. Closure waits for pending reviews and active upload slots. Owner-provided images always cost and earn **0 QC**; no zero-value or self-reward ledger entry is posted. The existing nonnegative wallets, immutable reservations, audited decisions, reward linkage and ledger append-only guards remain active.

## Capture, labelling and recovery

All three contexts use `QuickCapture`: personal owner, crowdsourced owner and community contributor. Explicit **Open live camera** requests video only, prefers the rear camera and keeps its stream across Save & Capture Next. Saving returns to the existing viewfinder; browser camera/file-picker restrictions are not bypassed. A device-camera picker and multi-image gallery are alternatives. Gallery does not request camera permission; switching from an active camera stops its tracks. Unmount also stops tracks and releases preview URLs.

The essential label uses large buttons with `aria-pressed`. Additional required criteria remain visible and validated; optional criteria are collapsed. Every image has independent values and a stable request key. Rights and privacy attestations cover the current mounted collection-capture session, while each saved image retains its own recorded attestation/timestamp/licence. They are not silently inferred from an old session or stored offline.

Save failure retains the image, request key and allocated slot. After allocation, label values are frozen for that retry. Interrupting a transfer can retry the same slot; My Submissions provides existing lease-based recovery/cancellation after navigation. Selecting a different image for an allocated slot requires discarding/restarting rather than changing its request provenance. Reloading does not retain local photo binaries or implement offline synchronisation. Server-side content/dimension/size validation, duplicate prevention, GPS/EXIF stripping and private access remain in use.

## Exports and privacy

Public versions reuse the existing worker/private cache and free download transport. New version fingerprints use export format 2 and include owner-provided/community-contributed provenance in immutable member annotations and per-image manifest records. Earlier format-1 snapshots remain byte-for-byte regenerable; the live pre-enhancement version was regenerated with its identical SHA-256.

Private export is an authenticated owner-only, disk-backed snapshot of 1–500 available personal images. It contains `images/`, `labels.csv`, `manifest.json`, `README.md` and `LICENSE.txt`, including the owner's private annotations, criteria version, per-image annotation revision and content hashes. It is not publicly listed or stored as a public dataset permission. It uses the existing safe canonical PNG conversion, generated UUID filenames, CSV escaping and ZIP entry helper. Private snapshots have a content-derived snapshot ID; they do not overwrite public immutable versions.

Bounds: 128 MiB per private archive, 120 seconds checked between images, 2 MiB each for manifest/CSV, existing four-download semaphore and 10 private preparations per account per throttle window (15 minutes). Temporary files close/delete on normal completion, failure or disconnect; no private ZIP is retained as a public object. Session/eligibility, image availability, label revision and removal restrictions are rechecked before each 256-KiB streaming chunk. A restriction or edit interrupts the archive; Content-Length and ZIP integrity expose truncation. Already delivered/in-flight bytes cannot be recalled. These are bounded pilot controls, not a completed load/decoder-isolation benchmark.

Owner removal uses the existing suppression/audit/purge path and revokes affected public exports without QC clawbacks. The upload cleanup worker now includes owner images and follows project-before-image lock order. Original source objects are not overwritten by exports or label edits. Software does not prove originality, consent, ownership or absence of visible personal information.

## Manual two-account/mobile acceptance

1. Start the existing stack normally. Confirm `0009 (head)`, healthy services and Mailpit at `http://localhost:8025`. Do not remove/reset volumes or re-seed account credits.
2. Use separate owner and contributor browser profiles, each with a verified `university.test` account. Record available/reserved wallets. Laptop webcam testing works on `http://localhost:3000`; an actual phone requires an approved reachable secure development origin for live camera. The default Compose ports remain loopback-only; no LAN/public exposure was added. Device/gallery picker behaviour varies by browser.
3. Owner: My Collections → Create collection → Collect images myself. Enter a name and one essential label question with choices (e.g. Round/Square/Rectangular/Other). Keep Private. Select the recorded licence deliberately. For additional criteria use Advanced criteria and optional metadata; questions default optional.
4. Create, open live camera, confirm rights/privacy, take a non-identifying photo, tap one label, Save & Capture Next. Confirm immediate return to the same viewfinder and no repeated consent form. Save another with a different label. Test Save & Finish, camera denial, retake and gallery selection without a camera prompt. Test portrait/landscape, touch targets, keyboard/focus, iOS Safari and Android Chrome physically.
5. In the gallery, filter by label, edit an unpublished personal image and save. In another tab attempt a stale edit; it should conflict rather than overwrite changed values. Export privately: inspect both image labels/answers, snapshot/revision information and stripped metadata. The other account and a guest must receive no image/gallery/private ZIP access. Both wallet balances must be unchanged.
6. Review publication. Confirm licence, privacy and the exact public field list; explicitly publish the policy. Opt your images into distribution through their existing gallery consent forms, then separately authorise them in Dataset publication and publish a version. A guest can download it free. Private text stays excluded. The other account still cannot contribute to this personal collection, even though its dataset is Public.
7. For a published personal image, editing requires first withdrawing its distribution grant. Confirm affected versions become unavailable, private source/QC remain, and private label editing works afterward. Remember the grant is terminal and old downloaded copies cannot be recalled.
8. Create a crowdsourced collection, target 2, reward 1 QC, with full requirements/licence. Publish: reserve 2 QC. Owner: Add My Images, capture/label/save one image; it is owner-provided, Approved, and earns 0 QC. Contributor: submit one image; it stays Pending. A third owner/community slot must be refused while the pending slot is committed.
9. Pause: new community capture is refused, owner capture is allowed only if capacity remains. Approve the pending community image: exactly one 1-QC reward. Repeated approval must not pay twice. Close: release only the unused 1 QC. Relative to the starting state, owner available is down 1, contributor up 1, owner reserved is zero. Closed capture/reopening must be refused.
10. Authorise both images for public distribution with their respective account's explicit consent and owner privacy review, then publish/download a version. Confirm owner-provided and community-contributed provenance, stable IDs, correct labels and zero download deductions. Pending/rejected/restricted images must be excluded.
11. In a separate funded test project, remove an owner image before closure. It should free target capacity without refunding or displacing a pending community commitment. New eligible community work can occupy the freed slot; normal rewards stay within the original reservation. No owner reward or automatic clawback appears.
12. Interrupt an upload; retry while its 15-minute slot is valid. Confirm one row/image, preserved labels and no double payment. Navigate away and recover/discard through My Submissions. Check oversized/unsupported images, required criteria and permission errors. Natural expiry, interrupted mobile/background sessions and prolonged sequential capture are remaining device acceptance checks; offline persistence is intentionally absent.
